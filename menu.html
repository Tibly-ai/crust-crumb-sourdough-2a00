Our Daily
Bakes

Every loaf we shape spends twenty-four hours cold-proofing to develop its signature open crumb and rich, custardy texture. We bake in small batches throughout the early morning. When the morning run is gone, the ovens rest until the next dawn.

Doors open at 7:00 AM Come early for the warmest selection

Today's Board

Our rotating selection highlights heritage grains, freshly milled flour, and slow-matured doughs. We source organic wheat from regional family mills to keep our footprint close to the soil.
