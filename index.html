The Bakery Hearth

We bake with the patience of old world hearth masters.

At Crust & Crumb Sourdough, we do not hurry the dough. Our starter, lovingly named Old Faithful, has been fed daily with local rye and spring water for over seven years. It provides the deep, complex lactic-acid tang that modern commercial breads left behind.

Every single loaf is scale-weighed, folded by hand, and cold-proofed in willow bannetons. When they hit our heavy stone deck hearth, the moisture locked inside expands to bloom open along our razor scores.

Our Daily Rhythm

The 36-Hour Sourdough Journey

01

Slow Autolyse

Flour and pure spring water rest together for two hours. This hydrates the starches completely before we introduce salt or our active mother starter.

02

Lamination & Folding

Instead of mechanically mixing, we fold the dough by hand every half hour over four hours. This gentler method preserves precious fermentation gas pockets.

03

Cold Retardation

Shaped loaves sleep in willow baskets inside cold rooms for twenty-four hours. Slowing the yeast allows organic acids to mature, deepening the sourdough flavor profile.

04

Hearth Bloom

Baked directly on dense volcanic stone slabs at 500 degrees with intensive steam. The crust carmelizes to deep bronze, leaving a crisp, glassy shell.
